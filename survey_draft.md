# Campus E-Waste Awareness Survey — Draft

**Purpose:** Learn what students and campus staff know about e-waste, how they have handled unwanted electronics, and what information would help them use formal disposal options. This is a short, voluntary pilot survey, not a representative study of the whole college or public.

**Estimated time:** 2–3 minutes

## Participant information and consent

We are conducting this survey for a student project about e-waste awareness and responsible disposal options. Participation is voluntary. Do not enter your name, phone number, email address, student or employee ID, or any other identifying detail. You may skip any question or stop at any time. Results will be summarized in groups and reported with the number of responses; individual answers will not be presented. The project team plans to delete the raw response file after the main project presentation. Please ask the project team if you have questions.

**1. Do you agree to take part in this survey?** *(Required; single choice)*
- Yes, I agree
- No, I do not agree *(End survey)*

## Questions

**2. Which best describes you in relation to the college?** *(Optional; single choice)*

- Campus staff
- Prefer not to say

**3. How confident are you that you know where to take an unwanted electronic device for responsible handling?** *(Single choice)*

- Somewhat confident

**4. Which is the best first step if you want to pass on an unwanted electronic device?** *(Single choice)*

- Open or dismantle it to separate materials

**5. Have you ever needed to dispose of or pass on an unwanted electronic device?** *(Single choice)*

- No

**6. If yes, what did you do with it?** *(Optional; select all that apply)*

**9. After seeing this project’s information, how likely are you to check a formal take-back or officially listed route next time you have an unwanted electronic device?** *(Single choice; use for a post-outreach version)*

- Somewhat likely
- Not very likely
- Not at all likely
- Not sure

## Administration notes for the project team

- Use questions 1–8 for the baseline survey. Ask question 9 only in a post-outreach survey, or use the same wording before and after if measuring a change in stated intention.
- Do not add fields that collect email addresses, names, account sign-ins, or device identifiers. Disable form settings that automatically collect email addresses.
- Before collecting responses, confirm the participant information, retention/deletion plan, and survey process with the project supervisor.
- Report the number of respondents and recruitment method. Treat results as a voluntary convenience sample; do not generalize them to all students, staff, or the public.
- Keep any open “Other” text answers brief and remind respondents not to include personal details. Consider removing free-text responses if the form platform makes them hard to review for identifying information.
